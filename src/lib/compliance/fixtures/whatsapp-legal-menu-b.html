WhatsApp Messaging Guidelines

Last updated: September 23, 2026

WhatsApp is a simple, secure and reliable way to send messages and make calls. These Messaging Guidelines (these “Guidelines”) apply to chats, calls, group chats, status updates, and communities.

Do not share illegal content

Do not use WhatsApp to share illegal content or to engage in illegal activity. You must respect the rights of other people, including their privacy and their intellectual property.

Respect people who ask you to stop

If someone asks you to stop sending them messages, stop. Sending messages to people who did not ask for them can lead to restrictions on your account.

  • Preventing further activity in chats and groups
  • Removing violating account information from our servers
