# WhatsApp Business Platform policy and spam enforcement

Updated: May 21, 2026

To maintain quality experiences at scale on the WhatsApp Business Platform, WhatsApp will enforce on WhatsApp accounts that repeatedly violate the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy/).

## How it works

Initially, WhatsApp accounts will get a warning with information on the policy they violated.

Depending on the number and severity of violations, accounts can be restricted or disabled. Restrictions last between one and thirty days.

## Webhooks

Subscribe to the [account_update](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/reference/account_update) webhook to receive notifications whenever a WhatsApp account has violated a policy.

## Appeals

If a business believes it is in compliance with WhatsApp policies, it can appeal the violation by requesting a review. Reviews usually take between 24 and 48 hours.

1. From the **Business Support Home** page, select the relevant Messaging account.
2. Select an option from the list of violations and click **Request review**.
