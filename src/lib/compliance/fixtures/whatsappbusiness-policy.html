WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

WhatsApp for Business enables conversational experiences between businesses and WhatsApp users. Creating valuable experiences relies on businesses following this policy.

Related: Ads that click to WhatsApp

Commerce Policy

The Commerce Policy is used to govern whether businesses can sell their products and services using WhatsApp. Businesses may not sell illegal drugs, weapons or adult products.

Opt in

You may only contact people on WhatsApp if they have given you their mobile phone number and you have received opt in permission from the recipient.
